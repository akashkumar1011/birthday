# 💖 Siddhi's 20th Birthday - Interactive 3D Greeting Card

A hyper-realistic, interactive 3D virtual birthday greeting card simulator and print-ready DIY keepsake created for Siddhi's 20th birthday.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Real 3D Card Posture & Physics**:
  - Starts standing upright in full 3D on a studio wooden plinth.
  - Smooth **"Lift Card"** and **"Lay Flat"** controls (or hold `Shift` + drag vertically).
  - 3D Flap folding wings (`152°`) casting realistic soft shadows with authentic cardstock thickness.
- **Cursor-Centered Zoom**:
  - Point your mouse cursor anywhere (top-left letter, pixel heart, photos, top-right letter) and scroll to zoom directly into that point.
  - Native 2-finger touch pinch-to-zoom on mobile devices.
- **Romantic Audio Player**:
  - Built-in soulful acoustic piano & strings love ballad: *"Can't Help Falling in Love"*.
  - Equalizer animation and volume control.
  - Custom song picker: upload and play any MP3 directly from your device!
- **8-Photo Stepped Pixel Heart Collage**:
  - Authentic photos rendered on luxury charcoal cardstock with natural skin tones, zero glare, and romantic stickers.
- **Personalized Handwritten Notes**:
  - Heartfelt birthday message on the left flap and blessing message on the right flap.

## 🚀 Live Demo & Deployment

This project runs 100% client-side with zero external build dependencies.

### Deploying to GitHub Pages:
1. Go to your repository **Settings** on GitHub.
2. Under **Code and automation**, click **Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, set **Source** to `Deploy from a branch`.
4. Select branch: **`main`**, folder: **`/ (root)`**, then click **Save**.
5. Your live website URL will be:
   👉 **`https://akashkumar1011.github.io/birthday/`**
